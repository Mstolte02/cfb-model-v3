04 / NO HIDDEN LAST STEP

From inputs to Power Rating.

  1. Compare two teams.

    Subtract opponent inputs from your team’s inputs. Home advantage is zero. Efficiency form is used only when both teams have at least two form games.

  2. Scale, weight, then convert.

    Each active difference is divided by its training scale and multiplied by its fitted coefficient. Add the terms, then apply the logistic function: 1 / (1 + exp(−sum)).

  3. Average the four predictions.

    Each model gets equal say. We average probabilities, not coefficients or logit scores.

  4. Repeat against every other team.

    The average of those neutral-site win probabilities is Power Rating. It is not the chance of beating a synthetic average team, winning the title, or winning the next game.
