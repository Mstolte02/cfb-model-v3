What the matchup audit found

The live model uses differences in offensive and defensive strength. Those terms can be rearranged into offense-versus-opposing-defense comparisons, but there is no explicit nonlinear O×D interaction in the active stack.

We tested both a nonlinear gap and an opposing-product feature. The product uses OA × DB − OB × DA. It changes sign when the teams swap. That lets a specific pairing matter beyond the individual team strengths. Standardized offense and defense are not predicted points.

Why not switch immediately?

The members are highly correlated (0.993–0.997). Learned blending helps in this sample, and combining live inputs into one logistic model also improves overall. But the multi-season prediction exports and original preseason frames are unavailable. This test holds the existing member fits fixed; it does not retrain the complete preseason models.

Next evidence needed: multi-season forward validation and prospective shadow results. The current production blend remains unchanged.
