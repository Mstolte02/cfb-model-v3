Total → member probability

Contributions add in log-odds within each member. Apply the logistic function to each member's total, then average the four probabilities to recover the ensemble. A positive term favors the home team. These are exact fitted contributions, not causal effects or predicted points.

What is interacting?

The offensive feature of the home team is paired with the defensive feature of the away team, and vice versa. We test passing versus coverage, rushing versus the front, protection versus pass rush, run blocking versus the front, explosive passing versus prevention, and after-contact running versus tackling.

Products use OhomeDaway − OawayDhome. Nonlinear gaps use g(Ohome − Daway) − g(Oaway − Dhome), with g(x) = x|x|. Both change sign when teams swap. Offense and defense scales are fitted on earlier games; each defense is oriented so higher means stronger.

WAR windows end at weeks 3, 6 and 9 and become eligible only afterward. Team unit features use the prior week's export. Rebuilt WAR fits its facets, normalization, player priors, conversion and update calibration before each target season. The inherited preseason inputs still retain retrospective upstream lineage; sources are not archived as-of-kickoff snapshots. This is not a fully prospective validation of the entire production model.
