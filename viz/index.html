Neutral-field Top 25

Ordered by neutral win rate — who wins today

Neutral win rate is the model’s power rating written as what it actually measures: play a team against each of the other 135 FBS teams on a neutral field, and this is the share of those games it wins. Ohio State at 90.2% beats a randomly drawn FBS opponent about nine times in ten. It is a round-robin average, so it carries no schedule — that is the other board’s job.
