Inside the résumé score

The Colley method adjusts winning percentage for schedule strength using this season’s FBS results. Opponent ratings and your résumé rating are solved together: beating a stronger schedule earns more credit. Conference, polls, scoring margin and preseason ratings have no effect.

Record base = 100 × (wins + half of ties + 1) / (FBS games + 2). SOS adjustment = 100 × FBS games / (FBS games + 2) × (average opponent rating − 0.50). Résumé score = record base + SOS adjustment. The extra win and loss give every team the same small-sample starting point. SOS rank 1 is the toughest played schedule. Ratings are an index on a percentage-like scale, not a forecast win probability, and can extend beyond 0–100.
