Scenario Builder

Play one season by hand. Click a team to make it the winner of any game on the 2026 schedule — everything you leave alone stays on the model’s favourite — and the conference champions, the committee ranking, the twelve-team field and the bracket all rebuild underneath you. The selection rules and the fitted committee weights are the same ones the Monte Carlo uses, so this is that machinery run on a season you chose instead of one it drew. It is an outcome, not odds; your picks stay in this browser and change nothing else on the site.

Team Ratings by Input

The Season Odds page ranks teams by what the model concludes; this ranks them by what it is built from. Sort on any column to ask a narrower question — best quarterback room, best offensive line, best defense, hardest schedule — against all 136 FBS teams. The rank on the left is always the team’s place in the whole country on the sorted column, so filtering to one conference tells you where its teams stand in FBS rather than renumbering them.

Players

Every projected two-deep slot in FBS, sortable and filterable across teams, conferences and positions, with each player’s rank in FBS and at his own position. Proj WAR is editable: change what you think a player is worth and the ratings, power ranks and matchup simulator all move with it. These are wins above replacement directly — summed over a roster they reproduce the team’s actual wins above a replacement-level team, so the team page shows the same figures.
